# What's new in Wivo 2.0

Wivo is now on your phone. You can work on a case together with a partner lab
or clinic. Files, photos and questions now stay on the job. Signing in is safer
too.

## Wivo on your phone

On your phone, open [app.wivodent.com](https://app.wivodent.com) and sign
in with your usual Wivo details.

- **Put it on your home screen** so it opens like an app. On iPhone, tap the
  Share button in Safari, then **Add to Home Screen**. On Android, tap ⋮ in
  Chrome, then **Add to Home screen**.
- See your own jobs, all active jobs, and jobs that partners share with you.
  Search by job code.
- **Take photos straight onto a job.** Photos taken in Wivo don't go into your
  phone's gallery and don't record your location.
- Read and answer questions on a job.
- **Notifications:** turn them on in the phone app's Settings to hear when
  something new arrives. On iPhone, this works once Wivo is on your home
  screen.
- For safety, the phone signs you out after 12 hours.

## Working with partners

You can now share a job with a partner lab or clinic, for example when someone
else designs or mills the work.

- An administrator connects your two organisations once, in
  **Settings → Partnerships**. Wivo then confirms the connection.
- When you create a job, choose **Assign stages to a partner** and pick which
  stages they do.
- The partner sees only what the work needs: teeth, material, shade, due date
  and files. On lab work they don't see the patient's name.
- Jobs that partners share with you are under **Partner jobs** in the sidebar.
  A partner can't change your job. If something needs to change, they ask in
  the job's comments.

## Files and questions on every job

Everything about a case now stays on the job.

- **Files:** drag photos, scans, X-rays and design files onto the job, or use
  **Add files**. Before upload, Wivo removes the location from photos and the
  patient's name and ID code from X-rays. Each file is named after the job
  code.
- **Comments and questions:** write to your colleagues on the job itself. If
  the job is shared, the partner sees the same comments. Tick
  **Ask as a question** and the job card gets a flag.
- Every job now has a **job code** (for example TAL-26-0042). Use it on the
  phone, with partners and on the case box.

## Two-factor sign-in

To keep patient data safe, signing in now takes two steps: your password, then
a 6-digit code from an app on your phone.

- The first time, Wivo walks you through the setup. It takes about two
  minutes. You can use Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or the
  Passwords app on iPhone.
- After that, every time you sign in, open the app and type the code shown next
  to Wivo.
- If you lose your phone or get a new one, ask your Wivo administrator to reset
  your two-factor sign-in.
