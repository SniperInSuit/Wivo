# Independent review of Run 2 (sql/089–090, push, the phone app)

On 2 November a separate reviewer read `f94633a..14f04c9` with fresh context
and checked each finding against the real migrations in PGlite. Migrations
089 and 090 are not on production, but files are never edited, so the fixes
are a new migration, **091**. Every fixed finding has a test that reproduces
the original problem and now expects it to fail
(`sql/test/run2ReviewFixes.test.ts`). Without 091, 9 of those 10 tests fail;
the tenth checks that restoring still works.

| # | Finding | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any signed-in user could stop phone push for every organisation. A push endpoint of just `https://` was accepted, and a direct INSERT accepted anything. The sender then threw on the bad URL and never marked a single push as sent. The same hole let one person point the sender at any URL, with no limit on rows and no timeout. | High | **Fixed, 091 and the sender.** The database accepts only the real push services (Google, Apple, Mozilla, Microsoft) and only through the save function, at most 10 phones per person. The sender checks the same list, pushes in parallel with a 10-second timeout, and a bad row can no longer stop the others. |
| 2 | Signing out on the phone, including the automatic 12-hour sign-out, used Supabase's default "global" scope. That signed the desktop out too, every day. | High | **Fixed.** The phone signs out only itself (`scope: 'local'`). |
| 3 | A deleted job could be shared again. The partner then saw it, but the owner could neither see nor end the share. An UPDATE with no WHERE clause could also mark jobs deleted without ending their shares. | Medium | **Fixed, 091.** Sharing a deleted job is refused, an UPDATE can't set `deleted_at`, and a deleted job takes no new files. |
| 4 | `deleted_jobs()` showed every deleted patient name to anyone with `jobs.write`, even someone who can only read their own jobs. | Medium | **Fixed, 091.** It now lists, and allows restoring, only jobs the caller could read. |
| 5 | Another account could take over a push endpoint (it had to know the endpoint's secret URL). | Low | **Fixed, 091.** The endpoint is refused. The app asks the browser for a fresh endpoint every time push is switched on. |
| 6 | Signed-out phones kept getting pushes: after the 12-hour expiry, and after "sign out on all other devices". | Low | **Fixed.** Both now remove the phone's push registration. |
| 7 | One e-mail and push per uploaded file: 15 photos from the phone meant 15 e-mails to each person on the other side. | Usability | **Fixed, 091.** One "files added" notification per job per burst (10 minutes, while unread). |
| — | `pending_pushes()` could interleave notifications, so the sender could not tell which one a full batch had cut short. | Low | **Fixed, 091 and the sender.** Rows come grouped by notification, and a cut-short one waits for the next run. |
| — | After a deploy, the service worker could cache `index.html` under the URL of an asset that no longer exists. | Low | **Fixed.** Only real files are cached. |
| — | The phone app had no frame protection. | Low | **Fixed.** `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and CSP `frame-ancestors 'none'`. |

## Left as they are (decided)

- **Owner-side changes to a deleted job by direct API call.** Someone in the
  owning organisation, holding the job's id, can still move the stage of a
  deleted job they can no longer see. It stays inside their own
  organisation. No partner is involved, because the shares ended at deletion.
  Blocking it would need a guard on every update of `jobs`, which would also
  fire on foreign-key cascades (deleting a patient). Not worth the risk.
- **Realtime and soft delete.** A deleted job leaves other open screens on the
  next refresh (window focus, about 30 seconds on the phone), not instantly.
  Realtime doesn't send an update whose new row the reader may no longer see.
  Acting on such a stale job now fails or does nothing. It can't leak or
  re-share it.
- **The 12-hour phone limit is enforced by the app.** On expiry the session is
  revoked on the server, but a refresh token copied off the phone before then
  would stay valid. Supabase's "time-box user sessions" setting enforces it on
  the server for everyone (RUN-2.md, optional).
- **Desktop sign-out stays global** (Supabase's default, as before). Signing
  out on the desktop also signs the phone out.
- **No full Content-Security-Policy** on the phone app yet. A script policy
  can't be tested without a browser here, and a wrong one breaks the app on
  deploy. It's listed in RUN-2.md for when the app runs on Pages.
