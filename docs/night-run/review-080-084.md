# Second independent review: sql/080–084, and what was done about it

080–084 were written after the first review (two of them fix its findings),
and production already had them or was about to. So they got the same
treatment: a separate reviewer with no stake in them, every finding confirmed
in PGlite. Fixes are in **sql/085**; each is a test in
`sql/test/secondReview.test.ts`.

| # | Finding | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 10-minute "new signup" window (083) was open to any owner — and anyone can found a clinic and become one; a removed owner kept seeing signups; an owner could rename a fresh signup or link another tenant's new worker first | Medium | **Fixed, 085**: 2 minutes, only an owner who is in a clinic, and only the link itself may touch a clinic-less profile. The race (another owner linking first within those 2 minutes) remains until owner-issued invite tokens replace the window; that is the next step for "Add worker". |
| 2 | `can_manage_payroll` still counted a grant from another clinic; a stale row blocked the rightful owner's grant | Low | **Fixed, 085** (own clinic only; granting replaces a stale row) |
| 3 | The partner's view of the thread used the author's current organisation; after an end, new owner messages still reached the partner | Low | **Fixed, 085** (organisation stamped on each message; nothing after the end) |
| 4 | `open_questions()` answered for jobs the caller cannot see | Low | **Fixed, 085** |
| 5 | Job number 10 000 in a year collided with 1 000; every insert after failed | Low (Medium for a big lab) | **Fixed, 085** |
| 6 | A code's year came from the client's `created_at`; a restore by INSERT renumbered jobs | Low | **Fixed, 085** |
| 7 | An old non-thread channel with a job id would break that job's thread and block deleting its creator | Low | **Fixed, 085** (detached, constraint validated) |
| 8 | Resuming a pause depended on the pausing admin still being a member | Low | **Fixed, 085** (the pausing organisation is recorded) |
| 9 | A removed worker still saw the names of channels they created | Low | **Fixed, 085** |
| — | The CSV import never sent `clinic_id`, so every import has been refused since 016/064 | (pre-existing) | **Fixed in the app** |

Also confirmed sound by the reviewer: none of 084's revokes breaks a policy or
an invoker function; every existing owner-side flow still works (add, remove,
restore, delete a worker; pay fields; own name; passwords; username login;
founding); thread scoping holds; job codes cannot collide across clinics;
nothing in 080–084 applies in PGlite but would fail on Supabase.
