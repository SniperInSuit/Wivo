# Wivo for the administrator (reception)

How to find your way around Wivo on the computer, one step at a time: patients, booking requests, the calendar and visits, clients, invoices and payments.

Words in **bold** are what you see on the screen.

Wivo is being updated. Steps marked *New in 2.1* are not in the version clinics use today (2.0.4) yet.

---

## What the owner turns on for you

You see only what the clinic's owner has allowed you in **Team**. For reception that is usually:

| To | You need |
|---|---|
| See patients | **View patients** |
| Change patients' details, notes and tooth chart | **Edit patients** |
| See the calendar | **View visits** |
| Book, change and cancel visits; booking requests | **Manage visits** |
| See invoices and payments | **View payments** |
| Make invoices and record payments | **Manage payments** |
| See clients (laboratories only) | **View jobs** |
| Add and change clients | **Edit jobs** *(New in 2.1)* |

- **Organisation admins** see invoices and payments without being given them. *(New in 2.1.)*
- **Patients** appear only when Wivo is set up for a clinic (WivoDental or WivoX). **Clients** appear only when it is set up for a laboratory (WivoLab or WivoX). The owner chooses this.

If something you need is missing from the menu, ask the owner.

## 1. Start here

### Signing in the first time
1. **Type your username or e-mail, and your password,** then click **Sign in**.
2. **Get an authenticator app on your phone** if you don't have one, for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Passwords on an iPhone. It's free.
3. **Scan the code** Wivo shows with that app. The app now shows **Wivo** with a 6-digit code.
4. **Type the 6-digit code** and click **Confirm**. The code changes every 30 seconds, so type the one showing now.

From then on, Wivo asks for your password and then a code from the app each time you sign in. **Lost your phone?** Ask the owner to reset your two-factor sign-in.

### Finding your way
5. **The menu on the left** takes you to each part of Wivo. You see only the parts you may use.
6. **The search box at the top** finds a patient or a job from anywhere: start typing a name.
7. **The bell at the top** shows your notifications. A red number means something new.
8. **New visit at the top** books a visit from any page.
9. **Overview** (first in the menu) shows today at a glance, including today's visits.
10. **What's new**, at the bottom of the menu, tells you what changed in the last update.

### Your settings
11. **Settings → Interface → Keel · Language** switches Wivo between Eesti and English. It follows you to every computer.
12. **Settings → Profile:** **Name shown as note author** is the name your patient notes carry.
13. **Settings → Security** lists your **Authenticators**, the apps that give your sign-in code. Add a second one as a backup, so a lost phone doesn't lock you out. *(New in 2.1.)*
14. **Sign out** with the door-and-arrow button at the top right when you leave the computer.

## 2. Patients

*Needs **View patients**; changes need **Edit patients**. Only in WivoDental and WivoX.*

### Find a patient
1. **Click Patients** in the menu.
2. **Type in the search box:** a name, the dentist, the clinic, a phone number or an e-mail.
3. **Narrow the list with Filters** if you need to:
   - **All dentists** or **All clinics**: one dentist's or clinic's patients;
   - **Without jobs**;
   - **Unpaid only** and **Sort: unpaid** need **View payments**. *(New in 2.1.)*
4. **Click the patient's row** to open their page.
5. **All patients,** at the top left, takes you back to the list.

### What a patient's page shows
6. **At the top:** name, date of birth and age, phone, e-mail, and the referring dentist and clinic.
7. **Treatment record:** clinical notes, allergies, material and shade preferences, bite and jaw joint. This is health data: share it only with those who treat the patient.
8. **Job history:** the patient's lab work. Click a row to open the job.
9. **Tooth chart:** each tooth's state (lab work, treated, healthy, missing).
10. **Notes:** notes about the patient, newest first.
11. **Invoices:** what was invoiced and paid, and the latest payments. It only shows; payments are recorded under **Invoices** (section 6). Needs **View payments**. *(New in 2.1.)*

### Add a patient
12. **Click New patient.** Wivo makes a new patient record straight away and opens it, named "New patient".
13. **Click Edit,** fill in the name and the details, and click **Save.**

If you clicked New patient by mistake, delete the record (step 19).

### Change a patient's details
14. **Click Edit** at the top of the patient's page. The details and the treatment record open for typing.
15. **Change what you need,** then click **Save**, or **Cancel** to leave everything as it was.

### Notes and the tooth chart
16. **Add a note:** type in **New note about the patient…** and click **Add note.** It's saved at once, with your name.
17. **Change the tooth chart:** click **Edit states,** click a tooth to change its state, then click **Save** (or **Cancel**).

### Export and delete
18. **Export for the patient** makes one file with the patient's record, jobs and files, for when a patient asks for their data. Wivo records that it was made.
19. **Delete a patient:** click the bin at the top, then **Confirm** within 5 seconds. This can't be undone; their jobs stay, without the link to the patient.

## 3. Booking requests

*Needs **Manage visits**.*

Requests come from the clinic's booking page on the web. Wivo doesn't reply to the person for you: you call or e-mail them.

1. **Click Requests** in the menu. A red number next to it shows how many new requests wait for a reply.
2. **Choose which requests to see:** **New** (shown first), **Confirmed**, **Declined**, **Spam** or **All**.
3. **Read the request:** the name, the time they picked or would like, their message, and what the price calculator showed them.
4. **Call or e-mail them:** click the phone number or the e-mail address.
5. **Book the visit:** click **Book.** The visit form opens with their name, the time and their message filled in. Choose the patient from the list (or **Create patient**), check the time, and click **Create visit.** The request moves to **Confirmed.**
6. **Not suitable:** click **Decline.** Declined requests are kept for 90 days, then deleted by themselves.
7. **Spam:** click the spam button. Spam is also deleted after 90 days.
8. **Clicked the wrong one?** **Back to new** returns the request to New.
9. **Delete a request for good:** click the bin. It's deleted at once, without asking.

## 4. The calendar and visits

*Needs **View visits**; booking and changes need **Manage visits**.*

### Look at the day and the week
1. **Click Calendar** in the menu.
2. **Click Visits** at the top to see only visits. In WivoX, **Combined** shows visits and lab jobs together.
3. **Choose Month or Week.** Month shows the day's visits as a list; Week shows them on a timetable.
4. **Today** jumps back to today. The arrows move a day or a week at a time.
5. **Click a day** to see its visits on the right, with a summary of the day.

### Book a visit
6. **Click New visit** at the top, or **Add visit** on the right. In the Week view you can also drag across the hours you want, or double-click a time.
7. **Patient:** start typing a name and choose the patient from the list.
   - **linked** means the visit is tied to the patient's record.
   - Not in the list? **Create patient "…"** makes a new patient record straight away.
8. **Fill in the rest:** **Visit type**, **Start**, **Duration (min)** and a **Note** if needed. The referring dentist fills in from the patient's record.
9. **Click Create visit.**

### Change a visit
10. **Open a visit:** click it in the day list or the Week view (in the month or on the right, double-click).
11. **Change what you need** and click **Save.**
12. **Move a visit** in the Week view by dragging it to another time. It's saved at once.

### When the patient comes
13. **Mark them in** with the buttons on the visit, on the right:
    - **Arrived** when they come;
    - **Handed over** when they leave with their work;
    - **No-show** if they didn't come;
    - **Cancel** if the visit won't happen.

    Each click is saved at once. **Restore** undoes it.
14. **Cancel or delete?** **Cancel** keeps the visit in the calendar, crossed out. **Delete** in the visit form (then **Confirm**) removes it completely. Prefer Cancel.
15. **Open the patient's page** from a visit with the arrow next to their name.

### Find visits
16. **Filter** (the funnel icon in the month) shows only some patients, kinds of work or dentists. The arrows next to it step through the matches.

## 5. Clients

*Needs **View jobs**; adding and changing clients needs **Edit jobs** too (New in 2.1). Only in WivoLab and WivoX.*

A client is a dental practice that sends the laboratory work.

1. **Click Clients** in the menu.
2. **Find a client:** type in **Search clients…** (name, contact person or e-mail).
3. **Add a client:** click **New client,** fill in the form, and click **Save.**
   - **Name** is required.
   - The **Email** you enter here is where the client's invoices are sent.
4. **Change a client:** click their row, change what you need, and click **Save.**
5. **A client you no longer work with:** open them and click **Archive.** They leave the list and the invoice form, but nothing is lost.
   - **Archive (n)**, at the top, shows archived clients again.
   - **Restore** brings one back.
6. **Delete** is offered only for a client with no jobs and no invoices: click **Delete**, then **Confirm delete.**
7. **CSV** saves the list on screen as a spreadsheet file. *(New in 2.1: Wivo records that the list was exported.)*

The **Invoices** column needs **View payments**. *(New in 2.1.)*

## 6. Invoices and payments

*Needs **View payments**; making invoices and recording payments needs **Manage payments**. Organisation admins have both. (New in 2.1.)*

### Find an invoice
1. **Click Invoices** in the menu.
2. **The four boxes at the top** add up the list: **Invoiced, Received, Unpaid** and **Overdue.**
3. **Narrow the list:** **All, Unpaid, Drafts, Sent** or **Paid.** Overdue invoices show the due date in red.
4. **Search** by invoice number or name.
5. **Click an invoice** to open it on the right: its lines, totals, status and payments.

### Make an invoice
6. **Click New invoice.**
7. **Bill to:** choose **Patient** or **Client,** then choose who.
   - Choose a patient from the list. **Create patient "…"** makes a new patient record straight away, so use it only for a new patient.
8. **Unbilled work** lists the patient's jobs not invoiced yet. Click one to add it as a line.
9. **Add line** adds a line by hand: **Description, Qty** and **Price.** The bin removes a line.
10. **Check the date and VAT.** **Payment due** follows from the date and the clinic's payment terms.
11. **Note on invoice** if needed. It is printed on the invoice.
12. **Paying in parts?** **Split into instalments** makes one invoice per month, all at once, plus a payment plan.
13. **Click Create invoice.**

Before you click it:
- **The invoice gets its number at once,** and the number is never used again. An invoice can't be changed afterwards: if something is wrong, delete it or mark it **Cancelled**, and make a new one.
- **If your clinic sends invoices by e-mail automatically** (the owner sets this up), an invoice dated today goes to the patient or client within the hour, even while it says **Draft**.

### Record a payment
14. **Open the invoice** (step 5).
15. **Type the Amount** received, choose how it was paid (**Bank transfer, Cash, Card** or **Other**), and check the date. A **Reference** is optional.
16. **Click Add payment.** When the whole amount is paid, the invoice turns **Paid** by itself. A part payment lowers **Unpaid**.

Always record money with **Add payment**: clicking the **Paid** status alone records no money, and the invoice still counts as unpaid. To remove a payment typed by mistake, click its bin; it's removed at once.

### Status, print and delete
17. **Status:** **Draft, Sent, Paid** or **Cancelled.** Click to change it. **Sent** is only a mark for you; Wivo doesn't change it when it e-mails an invoice.
18. **Print or save as PDF:** click the printer at the top of the invoice, then **Print / save PDF.** If the clinic's details are missing from the invoice, Wivo says which; the owner fills them in under **Settings → Clinic.**
19. **Delete invoice,** then **Delete permanently.** Its number is not used again.

### Payment plans
20. **Payment plans,** above the list, appear when an invoice was split into instalments. Click a plan to see each instalment, what's paid and what's late. Click an instalment to open its invoice.
21. **Stop plan** cancels the instalments not yet paid; money already received stays.

### Export
22. **CSV** saves the invoices on screen as a spreadsheet file. *(New in 2.1: Wivo records that it was exported.)*

## If Wivo says no

If a button you click is refused, you don't have the permission for it. Wivo says: "You don't have permission for this. Ask your clinic's owner." *(New in 2.1.)* Ask the owner to turn it on in **Team.**
